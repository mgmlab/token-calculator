Demo playbook: seven customers, seven different answers

A talk track for showing the calculator to our team and to clients. Each scenario is a realistic customer story that lands on a different recommendation, with a one-click link that loads it and one input to change live so the room sees the answer move.

How to position it. This is a conversation tool, not a quote. We use it to show a customer that the right answer depends on their usage, their constraints and who they are willing to buy from, and that we have looked at every option: buying servers, renting GPUs, paying per token and mixing them.

Numbers below were taken from the live site on September 29, 2026. API and GPU rental prices refresh daily, so dollar figures will drift a little; the verdicts should hold. Server prices are placeholders until we have quotes, which is why every scenario shows Confidence: Low. That is a talking point, not a problem (see the questions section).

Opening (about two minutes)

  1. The question every customer asks: “Should we buy servers, rent GPUs, or just pay per token?”
  2. The honest answer: it depends on how many people use it, how much text goes in and out, how fast it has to be, how busy the peak is, and who you are allowed to buy from.
  3. What the tool does: describe the workload once, and it prices every way to run it side by side, shows the math behind every number, and names the cheapest architecture with a confidence rating.

“Let me show you how different the answer is for different customers, and then we'll plug in yours.”

Questions to expect, and how to answer them

“Confidence says Low. Should I trust this?”

It says Low because server prices are placeholders and throughput is a theoretical estimate until we benchmark it. That is the next step we offer: current quotes from your preferred vendor and a measured run in the Pellera lab. The verdict usually holds; the ranges tighten.

“We get discounts from our cloud provider.”

These are public list prices on purpose, so every option is compared fairly. This is a direction-setting tool, not a quote. If a discount is large enough to change the answer, the Breakeven tab shows how much of a gap there is to close.

“Why not just use GPT or Claude?”

Those are different models, so they're shown for cost context only. Set Closed-model tier to Frontier and the Breakeven legend shows what a flagship model would cost (Scenario B: about $645K/yr against $86K for the hybrid). Whether a smaller open model is good enough is a question for our architects to test with you.

“How current are these prices?”

API and GPU rental prices refresh every day from public sources. Hover “Prices checked” in the header. Server prices are updated by hand from quotes.

“Can I play with this myself?”

Yes. Copy the share link and send it: it opens the same inputs, any options you removed and any prices or specs you edited, so a link works as a saved copy of the analysis. Or export the PowerPoint for the meeting notes.

Closing the conversation. End on their numbers, not ours. Click New analysis, open “How to gather these inputs from a client”, and walk through users, requests a day, prompt size, speed and business hours together. Agree on the two or three constraints that matter (approved providers, data residency, existing data center), and offer the next step: vendor quotes and a lab benchmark to raise confidence from Low to High.
